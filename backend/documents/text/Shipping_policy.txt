Shipping
What is delivery charge?
Delhivary charge is the charge which is charged by our trusted logistic partner to deliver the product to your doorstep. Sellers face large delivery charge for low volumn item and in such cases, charging a nominal delivery charge helps them offset logistics costs.

What is the expected delivery time?
Sellers generally procure and deliver the items within estimated 7-10 buisiness days of placing the order. Business days exclude public holidays and Sundays. Expected delivery time depends on following factors:
The seller offering the product
Product's availability
The shipping address
Sellers not liable for any delay in delivery by the courier company / postal authorities and only guarantee to hand over the consignment to the courier company or postal authorities within 0-7 days from the date of the order and payment.

Are there any hidden charges?
No, there are no hidden charges. All the listed product prices are final and inclusive of taxes. Delivery charges are not hidden, it will be shown (if there is any) in the invoice while checking out.

When delivery charge is free?
When the total order volumn is more than 500/-, we offer free delivery.

Why a product is not deliverable at my place?
Our delivery partners are working hard to cover all corners of pan India, some places are still out of their reach.
