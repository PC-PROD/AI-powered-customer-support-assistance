Cancellation & return
Cancellation
Customers can cancel their orders at any time before dispatch. Free cancellation can be done within 24 hours of placing the order. However after this period, if customers cancel their order, a cancellation charge will be imposed and rest of the amount will be transfered within 5/6 days to customer in the original payment method. This is to maintain a healthy relationship between seller & customer. Once the order is out for delivery by our logistic partner, cancellation charge will be imposed if product is refused upon arrival and and rest of the amount will be transfered within 5/6 days to customer in the original payment method. If an order is canceled by the seller due to unforeseen circumstances, a full refund will be processed for prepaid transactions. Sellers reserve the right to approve or deny (WITH PROPER CLARIFICATION) cancellation requests and may modify the cancellation window or waive fees at its discretion.

Return policy
Apparel items are eligible for return in the following conditions:
Return request is placed in 24 hours of receiving the item.
The product is in intact condition with MRP tags and unused.
The product is defective or differs in size or color from the ordered product.
Once the refund request is verified, the amount will be credited within 5/6 days in the original payment method.
